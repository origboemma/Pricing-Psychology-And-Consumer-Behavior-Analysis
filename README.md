## Pricing Psychology and Consumer Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using demographic and spending data. The objective is to identify the factors that influence customer spending and uncover characteristics of high-value customers.

The analysis focuses on the relationship between customer income, age, family size, and spending patterns. Insights from the project can help businesses improve customer segmentation, marketing strategies, and pricing decisions.

---

## Project Dashboard

The project includes exploratory data analysis and customer segmentation visualizations to identify spending patterns and high-value customers.

## Business Problem

Businesses need to understand which customer groups generate the highest revenue and what factors drive purchasing behavior.

This project answers questions such as:

* Does income influence customer spending?
* Does age affect purchasing behavior?
* How does family size impact spending?
* What characteristics define high-value customers?

## Key Findings

* Income is the strongest predictor of customer spending.
* Customers without children spend significantly more than customers with children.
* Age has a weak positive relationship with spending.
* High-value customers tend to be older and have higher incomes.
* Most customers belong to low and medium spending categories.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing efforts on high-income customers.
* Develop loyalty programs for high-value customers.
* Use customer segmentation to improve targeting.
* Create personalized promotions for different spending groups.
